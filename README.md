GRANTLY

Your future deserves a fair chance.

GRANTLY is a scholarship discovery and tracking website made for students in India. It helps a student find scholarships that fit their education, understand exactly why a scholarship does or does not suit them, prepare the right documents, and keep every application organised in one place.

The whole product lives in one file, grantly.html. There is nothing to install, no server to run and no account to create. Open the file in a modern browser and it works.


WHAT PROBLEM IT SOLVES

Scholarship information in India is scattered across many portals, notices and PDFs. Students often do not know which schemes they qualify for, which documents each one needs, or when a deadline is close. GRANTLY brings those answers together and explains them in plain language.

GRANTLY is an organiser and a guide. It does not award scholarships, it is not a government portal, and it can never approve or reject a student.


A NOTE ON THE DATA, PLEASE READ

The catalogue inside this project is illustrative demo data. The nine scholarship names are fictional. Their categories, such as Pre Matric, Post Matric, Minority, Girls' education and State government, reflect real types of schemes found in India, but the entries themselves are examples.

No official application links are included, because none were verified. Every card and detail page carries a Demo data label, and the footer reminds students to confirm eligibility, amounts, deadlines and application steps on the official provider website before acting.


FEATURES

The Intro Animation

When the site opens for the first time in a session, the GRANTLY wordmark appears on a near black screen, breaks into hundreds of small petal shaped particles, spreads through space, then gathers back into the wordmark. A lime line and the tagline appear, and the site fades in.
It draws sharply on high resolution screens.
A Skip intro button is always visible, so nobody is ever held back.
It plays once per browser session and does not replay when you move between pages.
If your device asks for reduced motion, the intro is skipped entirely.

The Landing Page

The landing page opens with the headline "Your ambition shouldn't be limited by your finances" and two actions, Find My Scholarships and Explore How It Works. Behind the headline sits a live constellation of floating scholarship cards, rupee markers and connecting lines around a glowing GRANTLY centre. It drifts slowly and leans gently toward your cursor.
A four stage journey explains Discover, Match, Prepare and Track.
A showcase presents sample scholarships with their award in rupees.
An eligibility example shows what a result looks like for a sample student.
An FAQ answers the questions students ask most, including whether GRANTLY is official and where data is stored.

Discover

The discovery page is a full search and filter workspace.
Free text search looks across title, provider, state, category, summary, education levels and fields of study.
Filters cover education level, state or Union Territory, scholarship category and minimum award.
You can sort by relevance, award amount or nearest deadline.
A switch limits results to scholarships that match your profile.
All filters work together and update the results immediately.
A live count shows how many scholarships were found.
Clear all filters resets everything in one click.
When nothing matches, a helpful empty state suggests what to try next.

Scholarship Details

Each scholarship has its own page showing the award amount, provider, category, eligibility with a line by line match check, the documents it requires, its deadline with days remaining, its location and the education levels it covers. A Save and track button adds it to your dashboard, and once saved the button shows your current status.

Eligibility Matching

Eligibility is explained, never guessed. For every scholarship, GRANTLY compares your profile with the real criteria and shows three kinds of lines: a tick for what you meet, a cross for what you do not, and a question mark for information you have not entered yet. The overall result reads Matches your details, Needs more information or Not matched, together with how many criteria were met. There are no random percentages and no hidden scoring.
The criteria checked are education level, state, field of study, annual family income, academic percentage and, only where a scheme requires it, gender.
Results are an informational guide. They are not an official decision.

Student Profile

The profile page is built around Indian students.
Name, state or Union Territory with all 36 listed, and district.
Education level from Class 9 through PhD and professional courses, including Diploma and ITI.
Field of study from Science and Commerce to Engineering, Medical, Nursing, Law, Agriculture, Design and Vocational studies.
Board, college or university, such as CBSE, ICSE, a State Board or an institution.
Academic percentage, checked to be between 0 and 100.
Annual family income in rupees, checked to be a whole number.
Gender is optional, with a Prefer not to say choice.
Clear error messages appear beside any field that needs fixing, and a confirmation appears when the profile is saved.

Dashboard and Application Tracker

The dashboard is a personal command centre, and every number on it is calculated from what you have actually saved.
Total tracked scholarships.
Applications in progress, meaning Applied or Under review.
Total awarded amount in rupees.
Your next upcoming deadline.
For each tracked scholarship you can set a status from Saved, Preparing, Ready to apply, Applied, Under review, Awarded and Not selected, write and save private notes, tick off documents with a live progress bar, open the full details, or remove it. Removing always asks for confirmation first.
Recommended scholarships appear when your profile matches ones you have not yet saved.

Document Checklists

Each scholarship generates its own checklist from its own requirements, so students are never told that every scheme needs every paper. Documents include identity proof, income certificate, previous marksheet, admission letter or bonafide certificate, fee receipt, bank account details and a passport size photograph. Your ticks are remembered. GRANTLY does not offer file uploads, so no fake upload buttons exist.

Indian by Design

All money is shown in rupees with the ₹ symbol and Indian digit grouping, such as ₹5,000, ₹1,00,000 and ₹12,00,000. One shared formatting function handles every amount in the product.
Dates appear as DD MMM YYYY and deadlines are counted in Indian Standard Time.
The language uses familiar Indian academic terms.
No foreign currency or international scholarship appears anywhere.

Look and Feel

GRANTLY uses a premium black identity with an electric lime accent, soft violet and champagne gold used sparingly. Headlines use Space Grotesk and body text uses Inter.
On desktop, a smooth custom cursor follows your pointer with a small dot and a softer ring that grows over clickable items. Main call to action buttons gently lean toward the cursor.
On touch devices the custom cursor is switched off completely, and the native cursor is never hidden.
Buttons, cards, inputs, dialogs and notifications share one consistent style, including hover, focus and pressed states.
A confirmation dialog and small toast messages give clear feedback after every important action.

Saving and Privacy

Everything is stored only in your own browser through local storage. Nothing is sent to a server. Your profile, saved scholarships, statuses, notes and document ticks all survive a refresh.
If the browser blocks storage, GRANTLY tells you instead of failing quietly.
Reset all my data on the Profile page clears only GRANTLY information, and only after you confirm.

Accessibility and Responsiveness

Semantic page structure with labelled form fields.
Visible focus outlines for keyboard users.
Progress bars that announce their value to screen readers.
Notifications that are announced politely.
Reduced motion support for both the intro and the hero.
A layout that adapts from large desktops down to small phones, with comfortable touch targets.


PAGES AND ADDRESSES

Each page has its own address after the hash sign in the browser bar, so so refreshing and the browser back and forward buttons all work.
The home address opens the landing page.
The how address jumps to How it works.
The discover address opens the search and filter page.
The match address shows eligibility results.
The tracker address opens the dashboard.
The profile address opens the profile and reset page.
The scholarship address followed by a scholarship name opens that scholarship's detail page.
Any unknown address shows a friendly Page not found screen.


HOW TO USE IT

1. Open grantly.html in a current version of Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.
2. Watch the intro or press Skip intro.
3. Go to Profile and fill in your details. Only the fields you want to share are needed.
4. Open Discover and search or filter.
5. Open any scholarship to see why it matches and what it needs.
6. Press Save and track.
7. Open the Dashboard to update status, write notes and tick off documents.


BUILT WITH

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript in a single file. No framework, no build step and no dependencies apart from two Google Fonts. The hero and the intro use the Canvas 2D API.


HONEST LIMITATIONS

The catalogue holds only nine illustrative scholarships and is not a live data source.
The hero uses Canvas 2D rather than Three.js or WebGL shaders.
Scroll driven storytelling, a step by step eligibility wizard, custom tooltips and a district dropdown are not included yet.
File upload is intentionally not offered.
This version has had a syntax check only. It has not yet been through a full manual test of every button, route, screen size and keyboard path, so please test it before sharing it widely.
